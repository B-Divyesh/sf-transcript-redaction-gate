Offline transcript redaction

Redact secrets before sharing transcripts

For support and engineering teams sharing terminal logs, it removes detected credentials while keeping diagnostic lines.

Sample result

See what redacted output looks like

The sample removes a bearer token and customer reference. Its receipt records detectors, lines, columns, and lengths.

Original transcript

Authorization: Bearer demo_token_value_1234567890
build completed in 1325ms
customer=CUST-12345678 status=failed

Redacted transcript

Authorization: Bearer [REDACTED:authorization]
build completed in 1325ms
customer=[REDACTED:policy:demo-pattern] status=failed
