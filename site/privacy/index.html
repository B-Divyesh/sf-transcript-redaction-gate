Policy sheet / effective 28 August 2026

Privacy, without fine-print fog.

Transcript Redaction Gate is built to process transcript content on your device. We do not receive it.

What stays local

The CLI has no telemetry or network code. It reads the files you direct it to and writes only the redacted output and receipt paths you choose. The browser workbench runs in your tab; transcript text and custom patterns are not uploaded or stored by us.

License data

If you purchase or restore the Team Kit, this browser stores the license token and its last verification result in local storage under keys beginning sb_license:transcript-redaction-gate. Verification sends only that token to the Sociobot billing API, at most once per day. Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record and processes checkout details under its own policy.

Analytics and cookies

This site ships no analytics, advertising trackers, third-party scripts, or cookies. Hosting infrastructure may retain short-lived security and access logs such as IP address, user agent, time, and requested path.

Control and retention

Remove locally stored license data at any time by clearing this site’s storage in your browser. Redacted files and receipts remain wherever you save them. We cannot recover transcript content because we never receive it.

Contact

For privacy requests, use the project issue tracker linked from the product page. Do not include transcript contents or credentials in a public issue.
